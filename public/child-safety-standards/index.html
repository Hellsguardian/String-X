Child Safety Standards

StringX is committed to maintaining a safe community and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

StringX strictly prohibits any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, or targets children.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish contact with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, sexual coercion, or threats involving a child.
  • Trafficking or solicitation of a child for sexual exploitation.
  • Requests, offers, sharing, or distribution of sexual imagery involving minors.
  • Any other conduct intended to facilitate the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child.
Strictly prohibited: Users must not create, upload, request, store, share, or distribute CSAM or any content that facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

2. User Safety and Reporting

StringX expects users to report suspected child-safety violations and other harmful behaviour as soon as possible.

Reports and safety concerns can be submitted to our designated child-safety contact using the contact information below. Where StringX provides an in-app reporting mechanism, users should use that mechanism for the fastest handling of reports.

We review safety reports and may take appropriate action, including removing violating content, restricting or suspending accounts, and preserving or sharing relevant information when required by law.

3. Handling of CSAM and CSAE Reports

If StringX obtains actual knowledge of content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we will take appropriate action consistent with these standards and applicable law.

  • Access to violating content may be restricted or the content may be removed.
  • Accounts involved in prohibited activity may be suspended or terminated.
  • Relevant information may be preserved as required for safety, legal, or investigative purposes.
  • Confirmed CSAM may be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate regional authority, as required by applicable law.
  • StringX may cooperate with law enforcement and other competent authorities when legally required or appropriate to protect children.

4. Prohibited Child-Exploitation Behaviour

StringX does not permit the use of the platform to facilitate or promote predatory behaviour toward children, including grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, or attempts to obtain sexual imagery from a minor.

Any attempt to use StringX for these purposes may result in immediate account restriction or termination and may be referred to the appropriate authorities.

6. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for using StringX lawfully and respectfully. Users must not attempt to circumvent safety controls, impersonate others, exploit minors, or use the platform to facilitate illegal or abusive activity.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate law-enforcement authority in your location.

7. Scope and Updates

These Child Safety Standards apply to StringX and its user community. We may update these standards when our safety practices, product features, or legal requirements change.
