StringX Privacy Policy

How StringX collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

StringX ("StringX", "we", "us", or "our") is a campus-focused social matching application designed to help users connect with compatible Garba partners and other participating students.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you when you use the StringX application and related services.

By using StringX, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and contact information

Depending on how you register, we may collect:

  • Phone number and verification information used for account authentication.
  • Name.
  • Account identifiers and StringX user ID/code.

2.2 Profile information

To provide the matching service, you may provide:

  • Gender.
  • Age.
  • Height and weight.
  • University and hostel.
  • Home state.
  • Course and academic year.
  • Interests and hobbies.
  • Garba experience/skill level and Garba preferences.
  • Partner preferences.
  • Answers to profile prompts.

2.3 Photos and verification information

If you use features that require a profile photo or identity/face verification, we may collect photographs or verification images that you choose to submit.

These images may be used to create your profile, perform the verification process, prevent misuse, and provide the matching service.

2.4 Technical and usage information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve StringX, such as device type, operating system, application version, IP address, log information, and information about interactions with the service.

2.5 Information you provide to other users

Information that you choose to place in your StringX profile may be visible to other eligible StringX users according to the app's matching and profile visibility rules.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your StringX account.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Create and display your profile.
  • Match users according to the application's matching rules and preferences.
  • Reveal or display matches at the time configured by the service.
  • Verify users and reduce fake, abusive, or fraudulent accounts.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve StringX.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and violations of our rules.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when necessary to operate StringX, including with service providers that process information on our behalf, such as hosting, database, authentication, storage, analytics, security, or infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of StringX, our users, or others.

5. User Profiles and Matching

StringX is a social matching service. Some information you submit may be displayed to other eligible users to enable profile discovery and matching.

You should avoid submitting information that you do not want other eligible users to see. Please do not include passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in profile fields unless StringX specifically requests it.

6. Photos and Face Verification

If StringX offers photo or face-verification functionality, images submitted for that purpose are used for the stated verification and safety purposes.

We will not use submitted verification images for unrelated purposes unless permitted by applicable law and disclosed to you.

Important: The exact retention period and technical processing of verification images depend on the implementation of the StringX service. Before publishing this policy, the operator should update this section to accurately describe whether images are deleted after verification, retained, or processed by a third-party verification provider.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain appropriate business records.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Before publishing: replace this general description with your actual retention periods where your implementation has specific retention rules, especially for profile photos and verification images.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.

You can contact us using the details below to exercise applicable rights. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your StringX account and associated personal information by using the account deletion feature in the app, where available, or by contacting us at the email address listed below.

We will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our operational retention requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

StringX is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. StringX is not intended for children who are below the applicable minimum age.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to StringX in violation of applicable requirements, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Services

StringX may use third-party services to provide infrastructure and functionality, such as authentication, database hosting, file storage, analytics, security, notifications, or other technical services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and our instructions or applicable contractual requirements.

Before publishing, the operator should list the actual third-party services used by the production version of StringX and link to their applicable privacy policies where appropriate.
